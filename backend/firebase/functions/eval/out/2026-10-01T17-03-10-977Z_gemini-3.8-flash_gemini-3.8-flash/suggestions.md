**First 25-hour airframe inspection.** Sling Aircraft recommends a comprehensive airframe inspection after the first 25 hours of operation.

Complete the initial 25-hour inspection checklist per paragraph 2.5 of the Sling maintenance manual and rectify any shortcomings.

**First 25-hour engine oil change.** Sling Aircraft advises performing an initial break-in engine oil change after the first 25 hours of operation.

Change engine oil and replace the filter element in accordance with the Rotax 915 iS manual.

**First 50-hour service.** Sling Aircraft recommends performing initial service requirements following the first 50 hours of operation.

Perform all engine and propeller service requirements in accordance with the respective manufacturer operating and maintenance manuals.

**50-hour / annual service.** Sling Aircraft recommends servicing engine and propeller systems every 50 hours or annually.

Perform all engine and propeller service requirements in accordance with the latest revisions of the Rotax and propeller manufacturer manuals.

**Condition inspection.** Federal aviation regulations require an annual condition inspection for experimental aircraft per operating limitations.

Condition inspection per 14 CFR Part 43 Appendix D. The Sling maintenance manual specifies an inspection interval of every 100 hours or 1 year, whichever comes first.

**Engine oil and filter element replacement.** Rotax and Sling Aircraft recommend replacing the engine oil and oil filter every 100 operating hours or annually.

Drain oil tank, refill with approximately 3 liters of approved oil, and install a new oil filter element. When operating with leaded fuel (AVGAS) more than 30% of operating time, oil change is recommended every 25 to 50 hours.

**Lubricate 100-hour / yearly lubrication points.** Sling Aircraft recommends lubricating flight control cable terminals, guides, torque tubes, and bearings every 100 hours or annually.

Lubricate rudder and pedal cable terminals, guides, elevator pushrod guides, torque tube bushes, trim tab hinge, aileron pushrod guides, flap torque tube bushes and actuator ends, rudder pedal bushes, control stick assemblies, nose gear support blocks, and main wheel bearings.

**Compass swing.** Sling Aircraft advises verifying compass accuracy by means of a compass swing every 12 months.

Verify by means of a compass swing the accuracy of the compass fitted in the aircraft.

**Altimeter & pitot-static test.** Federal regulations require the altimeter and pitot-static system to be tested every 24 calendar months for IFR flight.

Tested and inspected per 14 CFR 91.411. The Sling maintenance manual specifies carrying out pitot-static checks annually.

**Replace air ducting.** Sling Aircraft recommends replacing all air ducting every 300 hours.

Replace all flexible air ducting (CAT tubing, SCAT tubing, etc.) every 300 hours.

**Lubricate engine throttle cable.** Sling Aircraft recommends lubricating the engine throttle cable every 300 hours.

Lubricate the engine throttle cable with approved lubricant.

**Lubricate engine choke cable.** Sling Aircraft recommends lubricating the engine choke cable every 300 hours.

Lubricate the engine choke cable with approved lubricant.

**Lubricate heater activation cables.** Sling Aircraft recommends lubricating cabin heater activation cables every 300 hours.

Lubricate heater activation cables with approved lubricants.

**Mass & Balance check.** Sling Aircraft recommends performing a mass and balance check every 5 years.

Perform the mass and balance check every 5 years.

**Muffler / heat exchanger assembly inspection.** Sling Aircraft recommends disassembling and inspecting the muffler and heat exchanger assembly every 5 years.

Disassemble and inspect the muffler and heat exchanger assembly per paragraph 7.1.1 of the Sling maintenance manual.

**2000-hour airframe service inspection.** Sling Aircraft recommends a major structural and flight control service inspection every 2,000 hours.

Perform structural inspection per paragraph 2.5.2 during engine overhaul, including wing removal for spar root inspection, rudder and elevator control system checks, bell cranks, and landing gear plate inspection.

Replace main undercarriage attachment hardware.

General engine overhaul is due after 1,200 hours of operation or 15 years, whichever comes first. Extension of TBO by 5% or 6 months is permissible.

Replace air intake hose connecting the turbocharger and airbox.

**Initial 25-hour engine inspection.** Rotax recommends performing an initial break-in inspection after the first 25 operating hours.

Inspect newly delivered or overhauled engine at 25 hours per section 05-20-00, including differential compression test, spark plug inspection, magnetic plug check, hose security, and ECU memory readout.

**100-hour / annual engine inspection.** Rotax recommends a thorough engine check every 100 operating hours or 12 months.

Perform periodic engine inspection per MML-915 i section 05-20-00, including differential compression test, magnetic plug check, spark plug inspection, cooling and fuel system integrity checks, pop-off valve check, and ECU memory log readout. If leaded AVGAS is used more than 30% of the time, differential pressure check is recommended every 50 hours.

**200-hour engine wiring harness inspection.** Rotax advises inspecting the engine wiring harness and connections every 200 hours.

Inspect the wiring harness and electrical connections for secure fit, damage, and signs of wear in addition to the 100-hour checks.

**600-hour engine inspection.** Rotax advises inspecting electrical boxes and gearbox overload clutch assemblies every 600 operating hours.

Check ECU and fuse box mountings, inspect gear set for pitting, check tooth wear and function of overload clutches, and check intermediate shaft wear.

Remove and replace all spark plugs and verify gaps. If operating with leaded AVGAS more than 30% of total operating time, replacement interval is reduced to every 100 hours.

Inspect and clean screen in turbo oil sump. When operating with leaded AVGAS more than 30% of operation, clean screen every 50 hours.

Check the oil tank and clean if contaminated. When operating with leaded AVGAS more than 30% of operation, perform check every 50 hours.

Perform exterior engine cleaning. First performed at 25 hours, then every 100 hours.

**Replace carbon brushes (mini sliprings).** Airmaster recommends replacing mini slipring carbon brushes every 600 hours.

Replace carbon brushes in sensor-brush assembly at 600-hour intervals in accordance with procedure ASI-7-1-2 for mini sliprings.

Drain cooling system, flush circuit, and refill with approved coolant mixture.
